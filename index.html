How Cellarium works

  1. Pick a cell on the left — each one does exactly one thing.
  2. Paint it on the board. Most cells point somewhere: press R to rotate the brush, or right-click a placed cell to turn it.
  3. Press Run (Space). Every tick, every cell checks its rule and acts — or does nothing.
  4. Some cells make things (Generator prints Blobs), some break (Shredder eats what is ahead), some turn other cells, some carry pulses.
